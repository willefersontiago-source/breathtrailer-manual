# Solar Power System

This section explains how the **solar power system** operates in the BreathTrailer and how it contributes to battery charging during off-grid use.

The solar system is designed to work automatically and requires minimal user interaction.

---

## Solar System Overview

The BreathTrailer is equipped with a **roof-mounted solar panel** that charges the battery whenever sunlight is available.

Solar charging is managed automatically by the installed charging controller and does not require manual adjustment.

---

## Roof-Mounted Solar Panel

- Mounted on top of the trailer
- Charges the battery continuously during daylight hours
- Automatically starts and stops based on sunlight availability

☀️ Solar charging efficiency depends on weather conditions, sun angle, and panel cleanliness.

---

## Automatic Operation

- No switches or settings are required to enable solar charging
- The system automatically prioritises safe and efficient battery charging
- Solar charging can operate simultaneously with other charging sources

---

## Extra Solar Input (Solar Blanket)

An **Anderson plug** is installed on the battery box to allow connection of an **additional solar input**.

### Using the Extra Solar Input
- Connect the supplied solar blanket to the Anderson plug
- This increases total solar input and allows faster battery charging
- Roof solar and solar blanket can operate together

✔️ Extra solar input is recommended when staying off-grid for extended periods.

---

## Solar Charging Tips

To maximise solar charging performance:

- Park the trailer in direct sunlight when possible
- Keep the solar panel clean and free of debris
- Use the solar blanket to supplement roof solar when required
- Monitor battery level regularly

---

## Important Notes

- Do not connect unapproved solar panels or power sources.
- Ensure correct polarity when connecting external solar inputs.
- Solar charging may be reduced in shaded areas or during poor weather.

⚠️ Incorrect solar connections may damage the charging system.

---

Proper use of the solar power system helps maintain battery charge and supports reliable off-grid operation of your **BreathTrailer**.
