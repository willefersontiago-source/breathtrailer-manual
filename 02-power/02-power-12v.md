# 12V Power System

This section explains how the **12V power system** operates in the BreathTrailer and which components are supplied directly from the battery.

The 12V system is the primary power system used during off-grid operation.

---

## 12V Power Overview

All 12V power in the BreathTrailer is supplied **directly from the battery**.

The 12V system operates independently from camp site power and remains available as long as the battery has sufficient charge.

---

## 12V Powered Components

The following components operate on **12V power**:

- Interior lighting
- LED light bars
- Fridge (12V)
- Water pump
- Fans
- USB charging outlets (if equipped)

✔️ These systems are available even when the trailer is not connected to camp site power.

---

## Main 12V Power Switch

A main 12V power switch is located **inside the trailer box**.

### Operation
- Switch **ON** to supply power to all 12V systems
- Switch **OFF** to isolate 12V power when the trailer is not in use

Turning off the main 12V switch helps prevent unnecessary battery discharge.

---

## Battery Dependency

Because all 12V systems are powered by the battery:

- Battery charge level directly affects system availability
- High or continuous usage will reduce battery capacity
- Regular charging via solar or camp site power is recommended

---

## Power Management Tips

To maximise battery life and system reliability:

- Turn off unused lights and accessories
- Monitor battery level daily
- Use solar charging whenever possible
- Avoid running high loads for extended periods

---

## Important Notes

- The 12V system is designed for low-voltage DC operation only.
- Do not connect 240V appliances to 12V outlets.
- Do not modify wiring or connections without authorisation.

⚠️ Improper use or modification of the 12V system may cause damage or unsafe operation.

---

Correct use of the 12V power system ensures reliable off-grid performance and long-term durability of your **BreathTrailer**.
