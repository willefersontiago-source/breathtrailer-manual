## 🔌 Connecting the Gas Hose (Safe Connection Guide)

!!! warning "Gas Connection Safety"
    Improper gas connections can cause leaks, fire, or explosion. Always follow these instructions carefully.

### Step-by-step connection:

1. Ensure the gas supply is **completely turned OFF**  
2. Check the gas hose for:
   - Cracks  
   - Cuts  
   - Signs of wear or damage  
3. Connect the hose firmly to the stove inlet  
4. Tighten the connection securely **by hand or with an appropriate tool**  
   - Do not overtighten  
5. Connect the other end of the hose to the gas regulator  
6. Ensure the regulator is correctly attached to the gas bottle  
7. Slowly turn ON the gas supply  

### Leak test (mandatory):
- Apply soapy water to all connections  
- If bubbles appear:
  - Turn OFF the gas immediately  
  - Re-tighten or replace the hose  
- Never use the stove if a gas leak is detected  

---

## 🔥 Operating the Portable Gas Stove

!!! info "Safe Usage Guidelines"
    Always operate the portable gas stove in a well-ventilated outdoor area.

- Place the stove on a **stable, flat, and non-flammable surface**  
- Keep the stove **away from walls, tents, and flammable materials**  
- Never use the portable gas stove **inside the trailer or enclosed spaces**  
- Open the gas supply slowly before ignition  
- Ignite burners following the manufacturer’s instructions  
- Adjust flame using the control knobs as required  
- Always supervise the stove while in use  

---

## ⚠️ Important Safety Rules

!!! danger "Critical Safety Information"
    - Never use the portable gas stove indoors or inside the trailer  
    - Never leave the stove unattended while operating  
    - Keep children and pets away from the cooking area  
    - Do not move the stove while it is hot or in use  
    - Turn OFF the gas supply immediately after use  
    - Allow the stove to cool completely before storage  

---

## 🧼 Cleaning & Storage

- Allow the stove to cool completely before cleaning  
- Wipe surfaces with a soft, damp cloth  
- Do not use abrasive or corrosive cleaners  
- Remove food residues after each use  
- Store the stove in a dry, well-ventilated area  
- Disconnect the gas hose when not in use
