## ⚠️ Safety Instructions

!!! warning "Important Safety Information"
    - Always ensure adequate ventilation when using the stove  
    - Never leave the stove unattended while operating  
    - Keep flammable materials away from the cooking surface  
    - Turn off all burners and the gas supply when not in use  
    - Allow the stove to cool completely before closing the lid or cleaning  

---

## 🧼 Cleaning & Maintenance

!!! info "Care & Maintenance"
    - Clean the surface with a soft, damp cloth after use  
    - Do not use abrasive or corrosive cleaners  
    - Remove and clean trivets regularly  
    - Periodically inspect burners and gas connections  

---

## ❗ Important Notes

!!! danger "Gas Safety Notice"
    - This appliance is designed for recreational and camping use only  
    - Always follow applicable gas safety regulations  
    - If a gas smell is detected, turn off the gas supply immediately and ventilate the area
