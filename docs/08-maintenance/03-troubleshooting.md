# Troubleshooting

This section provides guidance for identifying and resolving common issues that may occur during operation of the **BreathTrailer**.

If a problem cannot be resolved using the steps below, contact an authorised service provider.

---

## No 12V Power

### Possible Causes
- Main 12V power switch is OFF
- Battery is discharged
- Loose or disconnected battery connection

### Actions
- Turn the main 12V power switch ON
- Check battery charge level
- Inspect battery connections and terminals

---

## No 240V Power

### Possible Causes
- Power selector switch in incorrect position
- Inverter is OFF
- Camp site power not connected
- Faulty power lead

### Actions
- Confirm selector switch position (Battery or City Power)
- Turn inverter ON if using Battery mode
- Check camp site power connection
- Inspect the 15A power lead for damage

---

## Inverter Shuts Down

### Possible Causes
- Inverter overload
- Battery voltage too low
- High-power appliance connected

### Actions
- Turn off connected appliances
- Remove high-power appliances
- Recharge battery using solar or camp site power

---

## Water Pump Not Operating

### Possible Causes
- Water pump switch is OFF
- Fresh water tank is empty
- Electrical connection issue

### Actions
- Turn water pump ON
- Check fresh water tank level
- Inspect pump wiring and fuse

---

## No Hot Water

### Possible Causes
- Water pump is OFF
- Gas cylinder is empty or disconnected
- Hot water system is OFF

### Actions
- Turn water pump ON
- Check LPG gas supply and connections
- Turn hot water system ON

---

## Fridge Not Cooling

### Possible Causes
- Main 12V power switch is OFF
- Battery charge is low
- Fridge settings incorrect

### Actions
- Turn main 12V power switch ON
- Recharge battery
- Check fridge temperature settings

---

## Solar Charging Not Working

### Possible Causes
- Insufficient sunlight
- Solar panel or blanket in shade
- Dirty solar panel
- Loose connection

### Actions
- Move trailer or solar blanket into direct sunlight
- Clean solar panel surface
- Check solar connections

---

## Awning or Shower Tent Issues

### Possible Causes
- Incorrect setup
- Obstructions or wind conditions
- Fabric packed away wet

### Actions
- Recheck setup instructions
- Pack away during strong winds
- Dry fabric thoroughly before storage

---

## When to Seek Assistance

Contact a qualified technician if:
- Electrical or gas faults persist
- Damage to critical components is detected
- Safety systems are compromised

---

Following these troubleshooting steps can resolve most common issues and help maintain safe, reliable operation of your **BreathTrailer**.
