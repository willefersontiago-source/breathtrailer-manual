# Kitchen Sink – Hot & Cold Water

This section explains how to operate the **kitchen sink** in the BreathTrailer, including both cold and hot water supply.

The kitchen sink is supplied by the trailer’s water system and requires the water pump to be operating.

---

## Water Supply Overview

Water to the kitchen sink is supplied from the **fresh water tank** or mains water connection, depending on the system setup.

The sink can provide:
- Cold water
- Hot water (when the hot water system is in use)

---

## Cold Water Operation

To use **cold water** at the kitchen sink:

1. Turn the **12V water pump ON**.
2. Open the kitchen tap.

Cold water will flow immediately when the tap is opened.

---

## Hot Water Operation

To use **hot water** at the kitchen sink, the following conditions must be met:

1. Turn the **12V water pump ON**.
2. Ensure the **hot water system is connected to LPG gas**.
3. Turn the **hot water system ON**.
4. Open the kitchen tap.

Hot water will be supplied automatically once the system is operating.

---

## Water Pump Requirement

⚠️ **Important:**  
The water pump must be switched **ON** for both cold and hot water to operate at the kitchen sink.

If the pump is OFF, no water will flow from the tap.

---

## Usage Tips

- Turn the water pump OFF when the sink is not in use to save battery power.
- Avoid running the water pump dry.
- Monitor fresh water and grey water tank levels regularly.
- Use hot water only when required to conserve gas and water.

---

## Important Notes

- Do not operate the water pump without water in the fresh water tank.
- If water flow becomes inconsistent, check the pump and tank levels.
- Ensure all connections are secure before use.

---

Correct operation of the kitchen sink system ensures reliable access to hot and cold water during your **BreathTrailer** trips.
