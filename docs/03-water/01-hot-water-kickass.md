# Hot Water System – KickAss - For Breath Max read paper manual.

This section explains the operation and safe use of the **KickAss 6L Portable Instant Gas Hot Water System** installed in the BreathTrailer.

The system is designed to provide instant hot water for showering and general use while camping or off-grid.

---

## System Overview

The BreathTrailer is equipped with a **KickAss 6L tank-less instant gas hot water system**.

Key characteristics:
- Instant hot water (no storage tank)
- Automatic ignition
- LCD temperature display
- Australian gas safety certified
- Portable and durable design

The system heats water only when it is flowing, providing hot water on demand.

---

## Key Features

- **Instant Hot Water**  
  Water is heated immediately as it passes through the unit.

- **Self-Igniting Operation**  
  The system automatically ignites when water flow is detected.

- **LCD Temperature Display**  
  Displays water temperature between **0°C and 45°C**.

- **Gas Safe Certified**  
  Certified to meet Australian gas safety standards.

- **Durable Construction**  
  Powder-coated steel body designed for outdoor use.

- **Shower Head ON/OFF Switch**  
  Allows control of water flow to reduce water usage.

---

## Water Supply Options

The hot water system can be supplied with water using:

- Mains water via hose connection  
- 12V pressure water pump connected to a water tank

⚠️ **Important:**  
The **water pump must be turned ON** for the hot water system to operate.

---

## Gas Requirements

- LPG gas cylinder is required (not supplied)
- The system uses a **new-style Australian-standard gas connection**
- Gas regulator and approved LPG hose are required

### Gas Connector Compatibility

Gas cylinders manufactured or tested after **01/04/2022** must use the new-style connector.

If an older gas bottle is used, it must be exchanged for a compatible cylinder at a swap-and-go centre.

---

## How to Use the Hot Water System

Follow these steps to operate the system:

1. Ensure the water source is connected (mains water or water tank).
2. Turn the **12V water pump ON**.
3. Connect the LPG gas cylinder and ensure the valve is open.
4. Turn the hot water system ON.
5. Open the shower head or tap.
6. Adjust water flow and temperature as required.

Hot water will be supplied instantly when water is flowing.

---

## Safety Guidelines

- Never operate the hot water system without water flow.
- Do not operate the system in enclosed or poorly ventilated spaces.
- Always ensure gas connections are secure before use.
- Turn off the gas supply when the system is not in use.
- Allow the unit to cool before handling or storage.

⚠️ Improper use may result in gas hazards, burns, or equipment damage.

---

## Maintenance & Care

- Inspect gas hoses and connections regularly.
- Keep the unit clean and free from dust or debris.
- Drain water from the system when not in use or before storage.
- Store the unit in a dry, well-ventilated area.

---

Correct operation of the hot water system ensures safe, reliable access to hot water during your **BreathTrailer** adventures.
