# Battery Charging

This section explains all available battery charging methods installed in the **BreathTrailer**.

The trailer is designed to keep the battery charged automatically whenever possible, reducing the need for manual intervention.

---

## Battery Charging Overview

The BreathTrailer battery can be charged using multiple power sources:

- Roof-mounted solar panel
- Camp site (City Power) electricity
- Optional external solar blanket

Charging is managed automatically by the installed charging systems.

---

## Solar Charging (Primary Source)

The primary charging source is the **solar panel mounted on top of the trailer**.

- The battery is charged continuously whenever sunlight is available.
- Solar charging starts and stops automatically.
- No user setup or configuration is required.

☀️ Solar charging operates only when sufficient sunlight is present.

---

## Camp Site Power Charging (City Power)

The battery can also be charged using **camp site electricity**.

!!! tip "Installed Battery Charger"

A **22A / 32A battery charger** is installed inside the trailer box.

!!! tip "How to Charge Using Camp Site Power"

1. Connect the supplied **15A power lead** to the camp site outlet.
2. Plug the other end into the trailer box power inlet.
3. Battery charging will start automatically.

✔️ The battery charger manages charging safely and efficiently.

---

## Charging While Towing

When the trailer is connected to the tow vehicle, the battery may receive supplementary charging depending on the installed system configuration.

---

## Charging Status

- Battery status can be checked on the battery box display.
- Charging occurs automatically based on available power sources.

---

## Important Notes

- Multiple charging sources can operate simultaneously (for example, solar and camp site power).
- Charging systems are designed to protect the battery from overcharging.
- Do not connect unapproved chargers or power sources.

⚠️ Incorrect charging connections may damage the battery or charging system.

---

Proper battery charging ensures reliable operation of all 12V and 240V systems installed in your **BreathTrailer**.
