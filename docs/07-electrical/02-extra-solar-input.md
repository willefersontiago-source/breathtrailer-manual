# Extra Solar Input (Solar Blanket)

This section explains how to use the **extra solar input** available on the BreathTrailer battery system.

The extra solar input allows faster and more efficient battery charging when additional solar power is required.

---

## Overview

The BreathTrailer battery box is equipped with an **Anderson plug** that allows connection of an **external solar input**, such as a solar blanket.

This system is designed to work alongside the roof-mounted solar panel.

---

## Purpose of Extra Solar Input

The extra solar input is intended to:
- Increase total solar charging capacity
- Reduce battery recharge time
- Improve off-grid performance during extended stays
- Compensate for limited sunlight conditions

---

## Compatible Solar Equipment

- Portable solar blankets supplied with the trailer
- Approved solar panels with Anderson plug connection

⚠️ Only use compatible and approved solar equipment to avoid system damage.

---

## How to Use the Extra Solar Input

Follow these steps to connect an external solar source:

1. Place the solar blanket or panel in direct sunlight.
2. Ensure correct orientation for maximum sun exposure.
3. Connect the solar blanket Anderson plug to the Anderson input on the battery box.
4. Charging will begin automatically.

✔️ No additional settings or switches are required.

---

## Combined Solar Operation

- Roof solar and external solar input can operate **at the same time**.
- The charging system automatically manages multiple solar sources.
- Battery protection systems prevent overcharging.

---

## Solar Charging Tips

To maximise performance:
- Position the solar blanket to face direct sunlight
- Adjust the panel position throughout the day if possible
- Keep solar panels clean and free from debris
- Avoid shading the solar blanket or roof panel

---

## Important Notes

- Ensure correct polarity when connecting external solar inputs.
- Do not connect unapproved power sources.
- Disconnect the solar blanket before towing the trailer.

⚠️ Incorrect connections may damage the charging system or battery.

---

Using the extra solar input improves charging efficiency and enhances off-grid capability of your **BreathTrailer**.
