# Double Door with Flyscreen

The BreathTrailer features a **double door system with flyscreen**, designed to improve ventilation, comfort, and insect protection while camping.

The flyscreen allows airflow into the trailer while keeping insects out, making it suitable for use in warm climates and off-grid environments.

---

## Intended Use

The double door with flyscreen is intended to:
- Improve airflow inside the trailer
- Allow natural ventilation
- Reduce insect entry when doors are open

---

## General Guidelines

- Ensure doors are fully secured before towing.
- Do not force doors or flyscreen mechanisms.
- Keep tracks and seals clean to ensure smooth operation.
- Close and secure doors during adverse weather conditions.

---

## Maintenance Notes

- Regularly inspect door seals and hinges.
- Keep flyscreen clean and free from debris.
- Do not use sharp objects near the flyscreen material.
