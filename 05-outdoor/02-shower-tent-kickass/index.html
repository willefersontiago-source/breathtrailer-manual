    Shower Tent – KickAss

    This section explains the setup, use, and care of the KickAss Camping Shower Tent Awning installed on the BreathTrailer.

    The shower tent provides a private area for showering, toilet use, or changing while camping or off-grid.

    Product Overview

    The BreathTrailer is equipped with a KickAss vehicle-mounted shower tent, designed for quick deployment and durable outdoor use.

    The shower tent can be used as: - Shower enclosure - Toilet/ensuite area - Changing room

    Key Features

    • Ultra-fast setup (approximately 7 seconds)
    • Vehicle-mounted design using supplied brackets
    • Heavy-duty 300D rip-stop polyester fabric
    • UV-resistant material with internal lining
    • Prevents silhouettes and sagging
    • Compact external storage bag
    • Adjustable hanging straps (1.6 m – 2.1 m)
    • Lightweight design (approximately 7 kg)
    • Double-sided zip for easy access
    • Internal mesh storage pocket
    • Weighted curtain with structural bottom poles
    • Corrosion-resistant 6063 aluminium alloy frame

    Setup Instructions

    Follow these steps to set up the shower tent:

    1. Ensure the trailer is stationary and the handbrake is applied.
    2. Unzip the shower tent storage bag.
    3. Fold the tent outward from the trailer.
    4. Allow the frame and fabric to drop into position.
    5. Secure the bottom of the tent using the supplied pegs if required.
    6. Adjust the hanging straps to suit ground level and trailer height.

    ✔️ The shower tent is now ready for use.

    Using the Shower Tent

    The shower tent can be paired with: - A 12V water pump - The KickAss instant gas hot water system

    This combination provides a complete off-grid shower solution.

    ⚠️ Ensure the water pump is ON and the hot water system is correctly connected before use.

    Pack-Up Instructions

    To pack away the shower tent:

    1. Ensure the tent is clean and free from debris.
    2. Fold the tent neatly toward the trailer.
    3. Allow the frame to collapse into its stored position.
    4. Zip the storage bag fully closed.

    ⚠️ Avoid packing away the tent when wet.
    If packed wet, open and dry the tent as soon as possible to prevent mould or fabric damage.

    Care & Maintenance

    To maintain the shower tent in good condition:

    • Clean with cold water and a soft brush only
    • Do not use detergents, solvents, or harsh chemicals
    • Allow the tent to dry completely before storage
    • Regularly inspect mounting brackets and fasteners
    • Store the tent securely in its storage bag when not in use

    Safety Notes

    • Do not use the shower tent in strong winds or extreme weather.
    • Ensure the tent is properly secured before use.
    • Keep the area around the tent clear of trip hazards.

    Correct use and maintenance of the shower tent ensures privacy, convenience, and reliable performance during your BreathTrailer camping experience.
