# Fridge Operation

This section explains how to operate the **12V fridge** installed in the BreathTrailer.

The fridge is designed for efficient off-grid operation and is powered directly from the trailer’s 12V battery system.

---

## Fridge Overview

The BreathTrailer is equipped with a **12V refrigerator**, with capacity depending on the trailer model:

- 30L or
- 60L

The fridge operates automatically once power is supplied and does not require connection to camp site electricity.

---

## Power Supply

- The fridge operates exclusively on **12V power**
- Power is supplied directly from the trailer battery
- The fridge remains operational even when the trailer is not connected to camp site power

---

## How to Use the Fridge

To operate the fridge:

1. Turn **ON** the main 12V power switch inside the trailer box.
2. The fridge will start operating automatically.
3. Adjust temperature settings on the fridge control panel if required.

✔️ No additional setup is required.

---

## Power Consumption

- Continuous fridge operation will draw power from the battery.
- Battery charge level affects how long the fridge can run without recharging.

To maintain battery charge:
- Use solar charging whenever possible.
- Monitor battery level daily.
- Avoid opening the fridge unnecessarily.

---

## Usage Tips

- Pre-cool food and drinks before placing them in the fridge.
- Keep the fridge well ventilated.
- Do not overload the fridge beyond its capacity.
- Secure items inside the fridge during travel.

---

## Important Notes

- Ensure the fridge is switched OFF during long-term storage.
- Do not use the fridge if the battery level is critically low.
- If the fridge does not operate, check the main 12V power switch and battery charge.

---

Correct use of the fridge ensures efficient cooling and reliable performance during your **BreathTrailer** adventures.
