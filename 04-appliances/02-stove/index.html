    🔥 Stove – Dometic PI8022 (2 Burner Hotplate)

    Only for Breath Max

    The Breath Max is equipped with the Dometic PI8022 two-burner hotplate, designed specifically for camper and caravan use.
    This compact and efficient cooktop delivers reliable cooking performance while maximizing bench space and safety.

    🧩 Features

    • Compact two-burner cooktop with improved cooking efficiency
    • Dual burners with strong and consistent heat output
    • Heat-resistant safety glass lid, protecting burners when not in use
    • Dual-purpose glass lid provides additional workspace when closed
    • Easy-to-clean detachable trivets
    • Stylish stainless-steel finish
    • Push-button Piezo ignition for easy operation
    • Duo-coloured control knobs for clear temperature adjustment

    🔥 Cooking Performance

    • Total heat output: 9.7 MJ/h
    • Left burner (Small): 3.8 MJ/h
    • Right burner (Large): 5.9 MJ/h

    This configuration allows flexible cooking, from gentle simmering to higher-heat cooking.

    📐 Specifications

    • Model: Dometic PI8022
    • Number of burners: 2

    Power

    • Burner 1 output: 1.1 kW
    • Burner 2 output: 1.8 kW

    Size & Weight

    • Width: 480 mm
    • Depth: 370 mm
    • Height: 80 mm
    • Net weight: 3.58 kg

    ⚠️ Safety Instructions

    Important Safety Information

    • Always ensure adequate ventilation when using the stove
    • Never leave the stove unattended while operating
    • Keep flammable materials away from the cooking surface
    • Turn off all burners and the gas supply when not in use
    • Allow the stove to cool completely before closing the lid or cleaning

    🧼 Cleaning & Maintenance

    Care & Maintenance

    • Clean the surface with a soft, damp cloth after use
    • Do not use abrasive or corrosive cleaners
    • Remove and clean trivets regularly
    • Periodically inspect burners and gas connections

    ❗ Important Notes

    Gas Safety Notice

    • This appliance is designed for recreational and camping use only
    • Always follow applicable gas safety regulations
    • If a gas smell is detected, turn off the gas supply immediately and ventilate the area
