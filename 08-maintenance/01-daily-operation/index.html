    Daily Operation Guide

    This section provides practical guidance for the day-to-day operation of the BreathTrailer to ensure safe use, system efficiency, and long-term reliability.

    Following these daily practices helps prevent issues and maximises off-grid performance.

    Daily Start-Up Checklist

    Before using the trailer each day:

    • Check battery charge level
    • Ensure the 12V power system is ON
    • Confirm water pump is ON only when required
    • Verify gas connections if hot water will be used
    • Check fresh and grey water tank levels

    Power Management

    To manage power efficiently:

    • Use 12V systems whenever possible
    • Turn the inverter ON only when 240V power is required
    • Switch the inverter OFF when not in use
    • Avoid running high-power appliances on battery power
    • Monitor battery levels throughout the day

    Solar Usage

    • Park the trailer in direct sunlight when possible
    • Use the roof solar panel as the primary charging source
    • Connect the solar blanket if additional charging is required
    • Keep solar panels clean for maximum efficiency

    Water Usage

    • Turn the water pump OFF when not in use
    • Monitor fresh water tank levels regularly
    • Avoid overfilling the grey water tank
    • Use hot water only when required to conserve gas and water

    Outdoor Equipment

    • Set up awnings and shower tent only in suitable weather conditions
    • Always secure outdoor equipment properly
    • Pack away awnings and tents if weather conditions change

    End-of-Day Checklist

    At the end of each day:

    • Turn off unused lights and electrical systems
    • Switch the inverter OFF
    • Turn the water pump OFF
    • Secure doors, awnings, and outdoor equipment
    • Check that the trailer is safe and secure for the night

    Travel Preparation

    Before towing the trailer:

    • Disconnect camp site power
    • Disconnect external solar panels or blankets
    • Secure all loose items inside the trailer
    • Ensure doors and awnings are fully closed and secured

    Consistent daily operation practices help ensure safe use, efficient system performance, and a trouble-free experience with your BreathTrailer.
