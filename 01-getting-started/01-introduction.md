# Introduction

Welcome to the official **BreathTrailer Owner’s Manual**.

This manual has been designed to help you safely and confidently operate all systems installed in your BreathTrailer without the need for technical assistance.

Please read this manual carefully before using the trailer for the first time. Correct operation ensures safety, system reliability, and long-term performance.

---

## Purpose of This Manual

This manual provides clear instructions for the operation, use, and basic maintenance of all major systems installed in the trailer.

It is intended for:
- Trailer owners
- Campers and off-grid users
- Anyone responsible for towing, operating, or maintaining the trailer

---

## What This Manual Covers

This manual includes detailed information about:

- Trailer coupling and safe towing procedures  
- Battery charging methods and solar systems  
- 12V and 240V power systems  
- Inverter and City Power operation  
- Hot water and water systems  
- Fridge, lighting, and electrical appliances  
- Awning and shower tent setup  
- Daily operation, maintenance, and troubleshooting  

---

## How to Use This Manual

For first-time users:
1. Start with the **Quick Start Guide** on the home page.
2. Review the **Safety Information** section.
3. Read the **Trailer Coupling & Handbrake** section before towing.

For regular use:
- Refer to individual sections as needed using the navigation menu.
- Follow all warnings and notes to avoid damage or unsafe operation.

---

## Important Notes

- This trailer is designed for camping and off-grid use.
- Always operate systems within their specified limits.
- Do not modify any installed systems unless approved by an authorised technician.

Failure to follow the instructions in this manual may result in equipment damage, unsafe operation, or loss of warranty.
