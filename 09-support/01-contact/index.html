    Support & Contact

    This section provides contact information and guidance for obtaining support, service, or technical assistance for your BreathTrailer.

    If you require help beyond the information provided in this manual, please use the contact details below.

    Customer Support

    For general enquiries, technical support, or service requests, please contact your BreathTrailer provider.

    Phone: 1300986843
    Email: info@breathtrailer.com
    Website: https://www.breathtrailer.com/

    When to Contact Support

    You should contact support if:

    • A fault cannot be resolved using the troubleshooting section
    • Electrical or gas systems are not operating correctly
    • Structural damage is observed
    • Safety-related components are affected
    • You require authorised repairs or replacement parts

    Service & Repairs

    • All gas system servicing must be carried out by a licensed technician
    • Electrical repairs should only be performed by a qualified professional
    • Use of unauthorised parts or repairs may void warranty

    Warranty Information

    Warranty coverage is subject to correct use and maintenance of the trailer in accordance with this manual.

    Damage caused by: - Incorrect operation - Unauthorised modifications - Improper electrical or gas connections

    may not be covered under warranty.

    Refer to your warranty documentation for full terms and conditions.

    Manual Updates

    This owner’s manual may be updated periodically to reflect product improvements or changes.

    Always refer to the latest version of the manual available online to ensure you have the most up-to-date information.
